# Asset and license index · مصادر الأصول

The release serves its assets locally. It does not contact a model marketplace, analytics provider or audio service during play.

| Photo-derived building/ground textures and sky | Poly Haven CC0. Individual works, artists and derivations appear in the [world credits](world.md). |
| Flags, lettering, buildings, cinematic plates and icons | Original project artwork with attribution and generation notes in [world credits](world.md) and [art credits](art.md). |
| Music, wind, footsteps and UI/game sounds | Original generated score and synthesis. [Sound credits](audio.md) |
| Amiri and Noto Sans Arabic | SIL Open Font License 1.1, served from the compiled Fontsource packages. [Amiri](../public/licenses/Amiri-OFL.txt) · [Noto Sans Arabic](../public/licenses/Noto-Sans-Arabic-OFL.txt) |
