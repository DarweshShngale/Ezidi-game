# الصوت والموسيقى | Audio and music

تتولد الموسيقى والمؤثرات لحظة اللعب من خلال Web Audio. تمتلك الفصول السبعة جُملاً لحنية أصلية قصيرة تتغير بحسب أحداث القصة. لا تتضمن الموسيقى ترانيم دينية أو تسجيلات من أحداث حقيقية.

The authored score, wind, surface footsteps, wood creaks, radio squelch, pulse and feedback cues are synthesized in the browser. The plucked-string model uses finite, cached Karplus–Strong responses at 22.05 kHz, a short filtered echo, and original chapter-specific note sequences. The soundscape requires no downloaded tracks, microphones, samples or third-party sound libraries. Sound generation and score data are original project assets distributed with the game source. The music is an original dramatic interpretation, not a recording of traditional Yazidi music.

Positional cues use an equal-power Web Audio panner and camera listener. Only a user gesture starts the audio context. Pausing suspends it and cancels the game's spoken line. Optional speech uses a locally installed Arabic system voice when available; subtitles are the authored dialogue, and recorded Iraqi performances are not included. Operating-system voices are neither bundled nor redistributed.
