# الرسوم السينمائية والأيقونات · Cinematic art and icons

The title painting and seven chapter plates in `public/art/` were made specifically for this game using OpenAI image generation. They depict fictional people and settings. They are illustrations, and do not document any historical individual, sacred site, or mapped route. Each chapter plate is delivered as a compressed WebP for local, on-demand loading.

The two PNG installation icons in `public/` are an original geometric design of a sunrise and an abstract ridge on a deep blue field, drawn for this project. The symbols remain inside the central maskable safe zone.

No third-party photographs, logos, font files, textures, or attribution-restricted media are embedded in these images.
